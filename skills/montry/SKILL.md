# montry-general v1.0.0

General skill for any user. No brand hardcoded.

## Priority
1. template-reels (LOCKED - wins over all)
2. motion-builder
3. qc-checker

## Fixed rules
- Video: 1080x1920, 60fps, SILENT - no voice, no music, no SFX.
- Motion: Reveal left to right, each word 0.4s, soft wipe before new scene.
- Ending: 4 items together until end, never delete any.
- If logo file missing, STOP. Do not delete logo and continue.
- QC: problems only.
- Output: real MP4 only. Never HTML as replacement.

## How to use
1. Edit skills/template-reels/brand.json with your brand.
2. Replace logo-360.svg with your logo 360x360.
3. Give script only. Do not re-explain ending.
